Everything a mess manager actually needs

Built around how a real mess is run

One-tap meal tracking

Mark breakfast, lunch or dinner for everyone in seconds. Had a guest over? Long-press to log an extra meal in that slot.

A real bazar list

Write down what was actually bought and by whom, instead of a vague "groceries" category no one remembers.

Automatic settlement

The meal rate and every member's balance recalculate the moment you open the dashboard — no spreadsheet, no manual math.

Only the meals you serve

Two meals a day, not three? Turn off the slot your mess doesn't use, right from Settings — it disappears from the tracker.

Private by design

Everything lives on the device — no account, no server, no one outside the mess ever sees the numbers.

Speaks your language

Switch the whole app between English and বাংলা any time, with light, dark, or system-matched theming.

Three steps, every day

From a tap to a settled balance

1

Add your mess and roommates

Name the mess, set your currency, and add the people sharing it — takes under a minute.

2

Mark meals and log bazar as you go

A tap for a meal, a note for a bazar run. It fits into the day instead of taking over it.

3

Open the dashboard, see the split

Balances, meal rate, and who owes whom are already calculated — no month-end reconciliation.

Built for the mess you actually run.

Not every household eats three meals a day, and not every manager wants to touch a spreadsheet. Mess Fellows adapts to yours.
